Closer is better

At Rễ, local sourcing is part of the recipe.

For us, “local” is not a marketing label. It means building a menu around the landscapes Vietnam already grows exceptionally well: the terraced northwest for tea, the central highlands for coffee, and Hanoi’s culinary heritage through cốm.

1.

Keep value closer to origin

Buying Vietnamese-grown tea and coffee helps more of the ingredient value remain within local supply chains and regional communities.

2.

Make provenance visible

We want guests to know not just what they’re drinking, but also the places behind it — Tây Bắc, ruộng bậc thang, the robusta fields of Tây Nguyên, and Hanoi’s cốm tradition.

3.

Design around the ingredient

Our drinks are built to reveal each ingredient clearly, rather than bury it under excessive flavouring or trends.

“A Vietnamese café should taste like the place it comes from.”

The sourcing principle behind Rễ

What guides us

Simple choices, closer connections.

We favour shorter supply chains, seasonal menu thinking, and long-term supplier relationships. The goal is a café experience that feels modern, grounded, and recognisably Vietnamese — from Tây Bắc teas and Tây Nguyên robusta to cốm inspired by Hanoi’s food heritage.

In practice

What local sourcing looks like.

  • Prioritising Vietnamese-grown jasmine tea, Shan Tuyết tea, robusta coffee, and culturally rooted ingredients such as cốm
  • Highlighting Tây Bắc, Tây Nguyên, and Hanoi as distinct places with different flavour and heritage stories
  • Working with traceable regional suppliers where possible
  • Sharing origin details in-store and online so customers understand what they drink
  • Using thoughtful packaging and service choices that reduce unnecessary waste
